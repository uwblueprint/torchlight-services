# FE-02 — Worker onboarding (`/onboarding`)

**Area:** Worker portal
**Depends on:** AUTH-01, DB-01 (`users`, `participant_assignments`)
**Blocks:** FE-03 (an invited account has to exist before sign-in can be tested), FE-10
(assignment management links here)

## Context

The flow board starts with onboarding before login. Torchlight staff invite a worker,
assign them a role, attach the participants they will work with, and walk them through the
privacy and receipt expectations. Today `src/app/onboarding/page.tsx` is a disabled form
beside a hardcoded five-step checklist, so it says nothing about whether any given worker
is actually onboarded.

This screen is admin-operated. There is no public sign-up: a worker cannot create their
own account, choose their role, or assign themselves a participant (decisions.md 8).

## Current stub

`src/app/onboarding/page.tsx` — disabled invite form (work email, display name, role) and
a static checklist. Replace both; keep the page reachable only by admins.

## Deliverables

1. **Invite form** — work email, display name, role (`passport_worker` or `admin`).
   Submit through AUTH-01's invite endpoint. Normalize email to lowercase and trim before
   sending; `users` enforces `email = lower(btrim(email))`.
2. **Inline validation** — required fields, email shape, duplicate account (surface the
   API's conflict), and a domain that fails AUTH-01's approved allowlist. Errors belong
   next to the field, with a summary that receives focus on submit.
3. **Onboarding status checklist driven by data** — account status (`invited`, `active`,
   `suspended`, `deactivated`), role, open `participant_assignments`, and the training
   acknowledgment. No local toggles that pretend completion.
4. **Assignment handoff** — link to the participant workspace (FE-10) for assigning
   participants. Do not duplicate that form here.
5. **Training acknowledgment** — record who acknowledged which material and when, as an
   audit event (AUDIT-LOG-01), not a checkbox in local state.
6. **Empty and error states** — no invited workers yet, invite failed, resend available
   per AUTH-01's flow.

## Out of scope

- Password setting, MFA policy, email templates, and the invite transport: AUTH-01.
- Participant profile or budget editing: FE-10.
- Any self-service path for participants or caregivers.

## Acceptance criteria

- An admin can invite a worker and sees the new `invited` record without a page reload
  showing stale data.
- An invited address that already exists is rejected with a readable conflict, not a
  silent second account.
- A non-admin who reaches the route is denied by the server-rendered guard, even when
  typing the URL directly.
- The checklist reflects server state: a worker whose invite is pending does not show as
  onboarded.
- Training acknowledgment creates an audit event with actor and timestamp.
- No email address, name, or role from source documents appears in fixtures.

## Verification

`npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`. Manual: invite with a synthetic
address against a local API; confirm duplicate rejection, non-admin denial, and that the
checklist changes only when the API says so. Do not send real email.
